# Governance

## Purpose

The FSI Toolbox is a curated discovery catalog. Source assets remain owned and
maintained in their respective repositories.

## Roles

- **Contributors** propose, improve, test, and document cataloged assets.
- **Asset maintainers** own the quality, security, documentation, and lifecycle
  of an individual asset.
- **Toolbox maintainers** review catalog changes, enforce contribution
  standards, manage releases and automation, and facilitate community health.

## Decisions

Routine changes are approved through pull request review by a toolbox
maintainer. Material changes to catalog policy, taxonomy, or governance should
be discussed in an issue before implementation. Maintainers seek consensus and
document unresolved tradeoffs in the relevant issue or pull request.

## Asset lifecycle

Assets use one of four maturity levels:

- `experimental`: early exploration; interfaces and guidance may change.
- `preview`: usable for evaluation with documented limitations.
- `production`: validated, documented, and actively maintained.
- `archived`: retained for reference but no longer actively maintained.

Asset maintainers should review their entries at least every 12 months. Entries
with unreachable repositories, unresolved critical security concerns, or no
responsive maintainer may be marked archived or removed.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes follow the same pull request process as other contributions
and require approval from toolbox maintainers.
